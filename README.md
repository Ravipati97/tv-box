# TV Box

Letterboxd, but for TV shows — search a show, rate it, and track what you've watched episode by episode.

**Live at:** https://ravipati97.github.io/tv-box/

## Using it

- **Sign in** — enter your email. New here? Pick a username and you're registered. Been here before? You're straight in. No password.
- **Home** — lands you on Now Watching (shows you've started but haven't finished), then your History, then a taste of what the rest of the group's been up to.
- **Search** — find any TV show from the Search tab.
- **Rate** — open a show and give it one star rating (half-star precision). Tap the same rating again to clear it. Each season has its own separate rating too, right next to its episode list — like IMDb/Rotten Tomatoes showing a season score alongside the overall one, for when a show's great overall but one season wasn't.
- **Track episodes** — mark episodes watched as you go; the show page shows your progress (e.g. "12/24 watched"), and in-progress shows automatically show up on Home until you finish them. Haven't started yet? "Start watching" on the show page adds it to Now Watching right away, no date to pick — it's a declaration that you're starting today. Marking an episode from a while back instead of just now? "Watched in the past" next to it lets you pick the real date. Episodes that haven't aired yet show an "Airs {date}" badge instead of a watch control, so there's no marking something watched before it exists.
- **Log old favorites** — already watched a show before you started using TV Box? "Mark all watched" (whole show or just a season) logs it in one go, with a date picker so it lands correctly in your history. Don't remember exactly when? Check "don't remember exactly when" and it's logged as watched a while ago instead of guessing a date. If some of what you're about to mark is already watched, it says so before you confirm — and a quick "Undo" appears right after, in case a tap was a mistake.
- **Watchlist** — save a show for later without starting it, from a bookmark toggle on the show page. Independent of your watch progress, so you can add or remove it any time. Browse it from your Profile.
- **Rewatch** — once you've finished a show, "Log a rewatch" records the date without touching your original watch history or progress.
- **New episode soon** — shows still airing get a heads-up: a "New episode {date}" badge on Now Watching, and a "Next: airs {date}" line on the show page.
- **History** — everything you've finished or rated, sortable by most recent, top rated, finish date, A–Z, or grouped by streaming platform. Shows up on both Home and your Profile.
- **Activity** — a group-wide feed of who finished or rated what, newest first and grouped by day like a shared diary. Filter it down to one person, or open any entry to see their full watch history for that show.
- **See what others rated** — the show page also shows everyone else's rating for it; tap it to see who rated what.
- **Where to watch** — the show page shows one clear answer for where it's free to stream (subscription or ad-supported, never rent/buy), based on your browser's region (data from JustWatch via TMDB). Wrong or missing? Anyone can fix it with "Not right? Fix it," which searches TMDB's full platform list and corrects it for the whole group until someone resets it back to automatic. That same answer also shows up as a small logo badge on posters across Home, History, and Search, so you can see where something streams without opening it.
- **Lists** — build your own curated lists (like "Comfort shows") from any show's page. Lists live at a shareable link and are visible to the whole group, the same as everything else here.
- **Members** — browse everyone who's registered and check out their diaries.
- **Compare with a friend** — from anyone's profile, see the shows you've both rated, your taste-match %, and your biggest agreements/disagreements.
- **Profile** — stats (including hours watched), a day-by-day Diary of everything you've rated, and the same sortable History tab as Home (tap a show for your watch history on it), plus tabs for your Watchlist and Lists. "Year in review" pulls those same stats into a per-year recap: shows finished, hours watched, your top-rated show, busiest month, and more.
- **Light / dark mode** — tap the sun/moon icon in the top bar to switch. It remembers your choice and otherwise follows your system setting.

Sign-in has no password or verification, so anyone who knows your email could sign in as you — fine for a small group of friends, not for anything sensitive.

If a passcode gate is enabled, you'll be asked for a shared code before you can reach the sign-in screen at all — ask whoever set up the site for it.
